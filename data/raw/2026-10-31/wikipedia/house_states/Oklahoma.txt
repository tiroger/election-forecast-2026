The '''2026 United States House of Representatives elections in Oklahoma''' will be held on November 3, 2026, to elect the five [[U.S. representative]]s from the [[U.S. state|State]] of [[Oklahoma]], one from each of the state's [[Oklahoma's congressional districts|congressional districts]]. The elections will coincide with [[2026 United States House of Representatives elections|other elections]] to the House of Representatives, [[2026 United States Senate elections|elections]] to the [[United States Senate]], and various [[2026 United States elections|state and local elections]]. The primary elections took place on June 16, 2026, and in races where no candidate receives over 50% of the vote, runoff elections would have taken place on August 25, 2026.<ref>{{Cite web|date=May 9, 2025 |title=2026 State Primary Election Dates |url=https://www.ncsl.org/elections-and-campaigns/2026-state-primary-election-dates|website= NCSL |access-date=5 August 2025}}</ref>

{{see also|Oklahoma's 1st congressional district}}
The 1st district is based in the [[Tulsa, Oklahoma|Tulsa]] metropolitan area. The incumbent is Republican [[Kevin Hern]], who was re-elected with 60.4% of the vote in 2024.<ref name="HPVT">{{cite web|url=https://www.cookpolitical.com/vote-tracker/2024/house|title=2024 House Vote Tracker|access-date=December 2, 2024 |website=[[The Cook Political Report with Amy Walter|Cook Political Report]]}}</ref> On March 11, 2026, Hern announced he would [[2026 United States Senate election in Oklahoma|run for]] the [[United States Senate|U.S. Senate]], following incumbent senator [[Markwayne Mullin]]'s appointment as [[United States Secretary of Homeland Security|secretary of homeland security]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gorman |first=Reese |last2=Luetkemeyer |first2=Em |last3=Huiskes |first3=Helen |date=2026-03-05 |title=Oklahoma Republicans Are Already Eyeing Markwayne Mullin’s Senate Seat |url=https://www.notus.org/2026-election/oklahoma-republicans-markwayne-mullin-senate-seat |access-date=2026-03-10 |website=NOTUS |language=en-US |quote=Rep. Kevin Hern will soon launch a bid for Senate, a source familiar with the matter told NOTUS.}}</ref>

===Republican primary===
The 2026 Republican primary in [[Oklahoma's 1st congressional district]] is the largest in the district's history with 11 candidates on the ballot. 13 candidates announced or filed, with Jackson Stalling announcing, but failing to file and Rob Durbin filing before withdrawing.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Krehbiel |first1=Randy |title=Crowded Republican primary largest ever for 1st Congressional District |url=https://tulsaworld.com/news/state-regional/government-politics/elections/article_826e63f8-df9f-453a-9eb1-d6df467e6e45.html |access-date=May 20, 2026 |work=[[Tulsa World]] |date=May 3, 2026}}</ref> In May, Tulsa pastor Jackson Lahmeyer received an endorsement from President [[Donald Trump]] ahead of the primary.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Krehbiel |first1=Randy |title=Trump endorsement of Lahmeyer doesn't deter CD 1 opponents |url=https://tulsaworld.com/news/state-regional/government-politics/article_b54ee45d-6692-4aa7-ac80-ee026e3b4c91.html |access-date=May 20, 2026 |work=[[Tulsa World]] |date=May 7, 2026 |language=en}}</ref> [[Folds of Honor]] founder Dan Rooney withdrew from the race after Trump's endorsement.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Krehbiel |first1=Randy |title=Rooney drops out of 1st Congressional District race |url=https://tulsaworld.com/news/state-regional/government-politics/article_d1adda5c-52ed-49f6-a1a1-f464df035e58.html |access-date=May 20, 2026 |work=[[Tulsa World]] |date=May 13, 2026 |language=en}}</ref>
