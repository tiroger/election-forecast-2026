{{ElectionsMO}}
The '''2026 United States House of Representatives elections in Missouri''' will be held on November 3, 2026, to elect the eight [[U.S. representative]]s from the [[U.S. state|state]] of [[Missouri]], one from each of the state's eight [[Missouri's congressional districts|congressional districts]]. The elections will coincide with [[2026 United States House of Representatives elections|other elections]] to the House of Representatives, [[2026 United States Senate elections|elections]] to the [[United States Senate]], and various [[2026 United States elections|state and local elections]]. Primary elections took place on August 4, 2026.

The 1st district encompasses the city of [[St. Louis]] and much of northern [[St Louis County, Missouri|St. Louis County]], including [[Florissant, Missouri|Florissant]] and [[University City, Missouri|University City]]. The incumbent is Democrat [[Wesley Bell]], who was elected with 75.9% of the vote in 2024.<ref name="HPVT">{{cite web|url=https://www.cookpolitical.com/vote-tracker/2024/house|title=2024 House Vote Tracker|access-date=December 2, 2024 |website=[[The Cook Political Report with Amy Walter|Cook Political Report]]}}</ref>

[[File:Missouri's 4th congressional district (since 2027) (new version).svg|thumb|270px|Missouri's 4th congressional district boundary used in the primaries of the 2026 elections<br/>{{maplink|from=Missouri's 4th congressional district (2027–).map|text=Interactive map version}}]]

The 4th District as used in the primary includes a sliver of [[downtown Kansas City]] between the Kansas-Missouri border and [[Troost Avenue]], historically the dividing line between the city's white and Black neighborhoods, as well as suburbs such as [[Lee's Summit, Missouri|Lee's Summit]] and [[Grandview, Missouri|Grandview]] in southern [[Jackson County, Missouri|Jackson County]], and rural counties as far south as the [[Springfield metropolitan area, Missouri|Springfield area]] and as far east as the [[Lake of the Ozarks]].

The 4th district as used in the general election is based in predominantly rural west-central Missouri, taking in [[Columbia, Missouri|Columbia]], [[Sedalia, Missouri|Sedalia]], [[Warrensburg, Missouri|Warrensburg]], and [[Lebanon, Missouri|Lebanon]].

The incumbent is Republican [[Mark Alford]], who was re-elected with 71.1% of the vote in 2024.<ref name="HPVT"/><ref name="da"/>

{{main|Missouri's 8th congressional district}}
[[File:Missouri's 8th congressional district (since 2023) (new version).svg|thumb|270px|Missouri's 8th congressional district boundary from the 2022 elections<br/>{{maplink|from=Missouri's 8th congressional district (2023–).map|text=Interactive map version}}]]
The 8th district is the most rural district of Missouri, taking in rural southeastern Missouri, including the [[Missouri Bootheel]], as well as the cities of [[Cape Girardeau, MO|Cape Girardeau]] and [[Poplar Bluff, MO|Poplar Bluff]]. The incumbent is Republican [[Jason Smith (American politician)|Jason Smith]], who was re-elected with 76.2% of the vote in 2024.<ref name="HPVT"/>
