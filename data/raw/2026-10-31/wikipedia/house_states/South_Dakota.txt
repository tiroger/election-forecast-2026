{{Elections in South Dakota sidebar}}
The '''2026 United States House of Representatives election in South Dakota''' is scheduled to be held on November 3, 2026, to elect a member of the [[United States House of Representatives]] to represent the [[U.S. state|state]] of [[South Dakota]] from its {{ushr|SD|AL|D}}. The election will coincide with [[2026 South Dakota elections|other elections]] held on the same date. The primary elections will be held on June 2, 2026, and in races where no candidate receives at least 35% in a primary, runoff elections took place on July 28.<ref>{{Cite web |title=South Dakota 2026 General Election |url=https://www.thegreenpapers.com/G26/SD |access-date=2025-06-13 |website=The Green Papers}}</ref>

Incumbent [[Republican Party (United States)|Republican]] representative [[Dusty Johnson]] was re-elected with 72.0% of the vote in 2024.<ref>{{Cite web |title=2024 House Vote Tracker |url=https://www.cookpolitical.com/vote-tracker/2024/house |access-date=2025-06-13 |website=[[Cook Political Report]] |language=en}}</ref>

[[Primary election]]s were held on June 2, 2026. Jackley received 79.2% of the vote against minimal opposition and Gronli ran unopposed for their respective party's nominations. Republicans have not lost a Congressional election in South Dakota since [[2008 United States House of Representatives election in South Dakota|2008]].
