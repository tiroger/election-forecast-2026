The '''2026 United States House of Representatives elections in Massachusetts''' will be held on November 3, 2026, to elect the nine [[U.S. representative]]s from the [[Commonwealth (U.S. state)|Commonwealth]] of [[Massachusetts]], one from all nine of the state's [[Massachusetts's congressional districts|congressional districts]]. The elections will coincide with [[2026 United States House of Representatives elections|other elections]] to the House of Representatives, [[2026 United States Senate election in Massachusetts|elections]] to the [[United States Senate]], and various [[2026 Massachusetts elections|state and local elections in Massachusetts]]. Primary elections took place on September 1, 2026.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Shook |first1=Ashley |title=Massachusetts legislature approves 2026 primary election date |url=https://www.wwlp.com/news/massachusetts/massachusetts-legislature-approves-2026-primary-election-date/ |access-date=September 24, 2025 |work=[[WWLP]] |date=September 22, 2025 |language=en}}</ref>

The 6th district represents northeastern Massachusetts and includes [[Salem, Massachusetts|Salem]], [[Peabody, Massachusetts|Peabody]], and [[Gloucester, Massachusetts|Gloucester]]. The incumbent is Democrat [[Seth Moulton]], who was re-elected uncontested in 2024.<ref name="HPVT"/>

Moulton's comments on [[transgender]] individuals following the [[2024 United States presidential election]] drew condemnation from local and national Democrats, including [[Governor of Massachusetts|Governor]] [[Maura Healey]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cbsnews.com/boston/news/seth-moulton-trans-athletes-maura-healey-backlash/|title=Gov. Maura Healey says Rep. Seth Moulton was "playing politics" with trans athletes comment|last=Riley|first=Neal|date=November 13, 2024|access-date=November 27, 2024|website=[[CBS News]]}}</ref> Combined with his short-lived presidential campaign in the [[2020 Democratic Party presidential primaries|2020 Democratic primaries]] and unsuccessful attempt to oust [[Nancy Pelosi]] as [[Speaker of the United States House of Representatives|speaker]] in [[2019 Speaker of the United States House of Representatives election|2019]], Moulton was seen as vulnerable to a primary challenger.<ref name="Garrity 11/27">{{cite web|url=https://www.politico.com/news/2024/11/27/moulton-lgtbq-congress-democrats-00191867|title=Moulton could draw challengers|date=November 27, 2024|access-date=November 27, 2024|last=Garrity|first=Kelly|website=[[Politico]]}}</ref> On October 15, Moulton declared [[2026 United States Senate election in Massachusetts|his candidacy for U.S. Senate]] in the Democratic primary against incumbent Senator [[Ed Markey]].<ref name=":0" />
