The [[2026 United States House of Representatives elections]] will be held on November 3, 2026, to elect representatives from all 435 [[List of United States congressional districts|congressional districts]] across each of the 50 [[U.S. state]]s. Five of the six [[Delegate (United States Congress)|non-voting delegates]] from the [[District of Columbia]] and the inhabited [[Insular area|U.S. territories]] will also be elected (Puerto Rico's delegate, the Resident Commissioner, serves four year terms and was last elected in 2024). Numerous [[2026 United States elections|federal, state, and local elections]], including the [[2026 U.S. Senate elections]], will also be held on this date.

==Election ratings==
===Latest published ratings for competitive seats===
Several sites and individuals publish ratings of competitive seats. The seats listed below are considered competitive (not "safe" or "solid") by at least one of the rating groups. These ratings are based upon factors such as the strength of the incumbent (if the incumbent is running for re-election), the strength of the candidates, and the partisan history of the district (the [[Cook Partisan Voting Index]] (CPVI) is one example of this metric). Each rating describes the likelihood of a given outcome in the election.

In total, there are 435 seats in the House of Representatives. 218 are needed for a majority while 290 seats are needed for a two-thirds [[supermajority]] (if all members are sitting and voting).

Most election ratings use:
*'''Tossup''': no advantage
*'''Tilt''' (sometimes used): very slight advantage
*'''Lean''': significant, but not overwhelming advantage
*'''Likely''': strong, but not certain advantage
*'''Safe''' or '''Solid''': outcome is nearly certain

==Party listings==
The two parties' campaign committees (the [[National Republican Congressional Committee]] and [[Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee]]) published their own lists of targeted seats.

===Republican-held seats===
On April 8, 2025, the [[Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee]] released their target seat list which included Republican-held seats and open seats.<ref>{{cite web |last=rice@dccc.org |date=2025-04-08 |title=DCCC Announces 2026 Districts in Play |url=https://dccc.org/dccc-announces-2026-districts-in-play/ |access-date=2025-08-25 |website=DCCC |language=en-US}}</ref> On December 10, 2025, the list was revised adding four districts (CA-48, FL-15, NC-3, and NC-11) and removing two (CA-40 and CA-41).<ref>{{cite web |last=iwata@dccc.org |date=2025-12-10 |title=DCCC Expands the Battlefield in 2026 with New Offensive Targets |url=https://dccc.org/dccc-expands-the-battlefield-in-2026-with-new-offensive-targets/ |access-date=2025-12-11 |website=DCCC |language=en-US}}</ref> It was revised again on February 10, 2026, where five more districts (CO-5, MN-1, MT-1, SC-1, and VA-5) were added.<ref>{{cite web |last=iwata@dccc.org |date=2026-02-10 |title=DCCC Announces Second Expansion of the House Battlefield for 2026 Cycle with 5 New Offensive Targets |url=https://dccc.org/dccc-announces-second-expansion-of-the-house-battlefield-for-2026-cycle-with-5-new-offensive-targets/ |access-date=2026-02-10 |website=DCCC |language=en-US}}</ref> A subsequent revision on July 9, 2026, added one district (FL-16) and removed one district (FL-15).<ref>{{Cite web |last=rbaron@dccc.org |date=2026-07-09 |title=DCCC Adds Florida's 16th Congressional District To Districts In Play As Democrats Expand Battlefield for 2026 Midterms |url=https://dccc.org/dccc-adds-floridas-16th-congressional-district-to-districts-in-play-as-democrats-expand-battlefield-for-2026-midterms/ |access-date=2026-07-22 |website=DCCC |language=en-US}}</ref> Another subsequent revision occurred on August 5, 2026, where eleven more districts (AR-2, CO-3, CO-4, FL-12, IN-5, NC-9, NE-1, NV-2, NY-1, NY-21, and TX-23) were added to the list.<ref>{{Cite web |last=jalango@dccc.org |date=2026-08-05 |title=90 Days Out from the Midterms, DCCC Announces Latest Expansion of the House Battlefield for 2026 Cycle with 12 New Offensive Targets |url=https://dccc.org/90-days-out-from-the-midterms-dccc-announces-latest-expansion-of-the-house-battlefield-for-2026-cycle-with-12-new-offensive-targets/ |access-date=2026-08-05 |website=DCCC |language=en-US}}</ref> One last revision occurred on October 2, 2026, where seven more districts (KS-2, MI-1, NC-13, NJ-2, OK-5, VA-6, and WA-5) were added.<ref>{{Cite web |last=jalango@dccc.org |date=2026-10-02 |title=Just Over 30 Days from the Midterms, DCCC Announces Eight New Districts to Our Offensive House Battlefield
