The '''2026 United States House of Representatives elections in California''' will be held on November 3, 2026, to elect the 52 [[U.S. representative]]s from the [[U.S. state|state]] of [[California]], one from each of the state's [[California's congressional districts|congressional districts]]. The elections will coincide with [[2026 United States House of Representatives elections|other elections]] to the House of Representatives, [[2026 United States Senate elections|elections]] to the [[United States Senate]], and various [[2026 United States elections|state and local elections]]. The passage of [[2025 California Proposition 50|Proposition 50 in 2025]] significantly altered many districts. The primary elections took place on June 2, 2026.<ref>{{#invoke:cite|web|title=2026 California Primary |url=https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/upcoming-elections/primary-election-june-2-2026 |access-date=September 24, 2025 |publisher=California Secretary of State}}</ref>

==Redistricting==
===Background===
In November 2025, votes decisively approved a [[2025 California Proposition 50|constitutional amendment]] to allow for mid-decade redistricting and to instate a map that would have likely given Democrats 5 additional seats in the [[United States House of Representatives|House of Representatives]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://elections.cdn.sos.ca.gov/sov/2025-special/sov/complete-sov.pdf|title=STATEMENT OF VOTE NOVEMBER 4, 2025, STATEWIDE SPECIAL ELECTION|website=[[California Secretary of State]]}}</ref> The map defined in Proposition 50 is a Democratic [[Gerrymandering in the United States|gerrymander]] intended to offset the [[2025 Texas redistricting|gerrymander by Texas Republicans]], both of which are part of the broader [[2025–2026 United States redistricting]] effort. It redraws several congressional districts to incorporate larger shares of urban and suburban Democratic voters, increasing Democratic registration advantages in competitive districts and converting several Republican-leaning seats into Democratic-leaning ones.<ref name="PoliticoLaunch">{{cite web |last1=White |first1=Jeremy |last2=Schultheis |first2=Emily |title='We can't stand back' — Gavin Newsom launches California gerrymander campaign |url=https://www.politico.com/news/2025/08/14/newsom-democrats-launch-california-redistricting-campaign-00509930 |website=Politico |date=August 14, 2025 |access-date=14 August 2025}}</ref> Republicans have responded to Proposition 50 with legislation, their own propositions, and litigation. Republicans challenged the proposition in court both before the election, as well as after it was passed by the voters, but their legal efforts were exhausted when the [[Supreme Court of the United States]] refused to hear the appeal.
