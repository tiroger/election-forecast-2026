The '''2026 United States House of Representatives elections in Maryland''' will be held on November 3, 2026, to elect the eight [[U.S. representative]]s from the [[U.S. state|state]] of [[Maryland]], one from each of the state's eight [[Maryland's congressional districts|congressional districts]]. The elections will coincide with [[2026 United States House of Representatives elections|other elections]] to the House of Representatives and various [[2026 United States elections#State elections|state]] and [[2026 United States elections#Local elections|local elections]]. The primary elections took place on June 23, 2026.<ref>{{Cite web|date=May 9, 2025 |title=2026 State Primary Election Dates |url=https://www.ncsl.org/elections-and-campaigns/2026-state-primary-election-dates|website= NCSL |access-date=5 August 2025}}</ref>

== Failed redistricting attempt ==
In November 2025, as a part of the broader [[2025–2026 United States redistricting]], Governor [[Wes Moore]] announced the formation of the Governor's Redistricting Advisory Commission, which was tasked with holding public hearings and providing recommendations for a new congressional map.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Sears |first1=Bryan P. |title=Moore forges ahead with redistricting effort, announcing advisory commission |url=https://marylandmatters.org/2025/11/04/moore-forges-ahead-with-redistricting-effort-announcing-advisory-commission/ |access-date=November 4, 2025 |work=Maryland Matters |date=November 4, 2025}}</ref> On January 20, 2026, the commission voted to recommend a congressional map that would redraw [[Maryland's 1st congressional district]] to improve the Democratic Party's chances of winning it.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Wood |first1=Pamela |title=Maryland commission recommends new congressional boundaries favoring Democrats |url=https://www.thebanner.com/politics-power/state-government/maryland-congressional-redistricting-vote-CFYGKDSGN5CD7NTAUEDP4ZAXQ4/ |access-date=January 20, 2026 |work=[[The Baltimore Banner]] |date=January 20, 2026 |language=en}}</ref> The map passed the [[Maryland House of Delegates]] by a 99–37 vote on February 2, 2026,<ref>{{cite news |last1=Wood |first1=Pamela |title=Maryland House approves congressional map; expected to stall in Senate |url=https://www.thebanner.com/politics-power/state-government/maryland-house-redistricting-vote-JOAA2YPSCJE5DLTUQKDLSH3ZW4/ |access-date=February 2, 2026 |work=[[The Baltimore Banner]] |date=February 2, 2026 |language=en}}</ref> but died in the [[Maryland Senate]] after Senate President [[Bill Ferguson (politician)|Bill Ferguson]] refused to hold a vote on the redistricting bill in the Senate.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Gaskill |first1=Hannah |title=Is MD's midcycle redistricting battle over? Yes, Ferguson says. |url=https://thedailyrecord.com/2026/02/20/maryland-opens-midcycle-redistricting-closed/ |access-date=February 20, 2026 |work=[[The Daily Record (Maryland)|The Daily Record]] |date=February 20, 2026}}</ref> In August 2026, the Maryland General Assembly convened for a special session, during which it passed [[2026 Maryland Question 3|Question 3]], which would exempt Maryland's congressional districts from guidelines in the [[Constitution of Maryland]] that require legislative districts to respect natural boundaries and the boundaries of political subdivisions. If approved by voters, it may allow Democrats to redraw its congressional maps ahead of the 2028 elections.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Gregory |first1=Matt |title=Maryland legislators approved a 'redistricting' referendum |url=https://www.wusa9.com/article/news/politics/maryland-house-sentae-approves-redistricting-ballot/65-59680979-7185-4501-8021-389586c1aa1b |access-date=September 12, 2026 |work=[[WUSA (TV)]] |date=September 12, 2026}}</ref>

{{see also|Maryland's 5th congressional district}}
The 5th district is based in [[southern Maryland]], and encompasses [[Charles County, Maryland|Charles]], [[St. Mary's County, Maryland|St. Mary's]], [[Calvert County, Maryland|Calvert]] counties and a small portion of southern [[Anne Arundel County]], as well as the [[Washington, D.C.]] suburbs of [[Bowie, Maryland|Bowie]] and [[Greater Upper Marlboro, Maryland|Upper Marlboro]].<ref name = "valid source"/> Incumbent Democrat [[Steny Hoyer]], who was re-elected with 67.8% of the vote in 2024,<ref name="HPVT"/> announced in early January that he would not run for re-election.<ref name=StWi/>

The 6th district is based in [[western Maryland]]. It covers all of [[Garrett County, Maryland|Garrett]], [[Allegany County, Maryland|Allegany]], [[Washington County, Maryland|Washington]], and [[Frederick County, Maryland|Frederick]] counties, and extends south into the [[Washington, D.C.]] suburbs in [[Montgomery County, Maryland|Montgomery County]], including [[Germantown, Maryland|Germantown]] and [[Gaithersburg, Maryland|Gaithersburg]].<ref name = "valid source"/> The incumbent is Democrat [[April McClain Delaney]], who was elected with 53.0% of the vote in 2024.<ref name="HPVT"/>
