{{Elections in Montana}}
The '''2026 United States House of Representatives elections in Montana''' will be held on November 3, 2026, to elect the two [[U.S. representative]]s from the [[U.S. state|state]] of [[Montana]], one from each of the state's [[Montana's congressional districts|congressional districts]]. The elections will coincide with [[2026 United States House of Representatives elections|other elections]] to the House of Representatives, [[2026 United States Senate elections|elections]] to the [[United States Senate]], and various [[2026 United States elections|state and local elections]]. The primary elections took place on June 2, 2026.<ref>{{Cite web|date=May 9, 2025 |title=2026 State Primary Election Dates |url=https://www.ncsl.org/elections-and-campaigns/2026-state-primary-election-dates|website= NCSL |access-date=5 August 2025}}</ref>

The 1st district is based in western Montana, including [[Missoula, Montana|Missoula]], [[Bozeman, Montana|Bozeman]], [[Butte, Montana|Butte]], and [[Kalispell, Montana|Kalispell]]. The incumbent is Republican [[Ryan Zinke]], who was re-elected with 52.3% of the vote in 2024.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=2024 House Vote Tracker |url=https://www.cookpolitical.com/vote-tracker/2024/house |access-date=2025-06-13 |website=Cook Political Report |language=en}}</ref> On March 2, 2026, Zinke announced that he would retire once his present term ends.<ref name=retired>{{cite web |last1=Anchor |first1=Bradley Warren NonStop Local |title=Montana Congressman Ryan Zinke to retire, will not seek reelection |url=https://www.kulr8.com/elections/montana-congressman-ryan-zinke-to-retire-will-not-seek-reelection/article_ef317ebf-4a81-588a-975c-18e7515b9cbc.html |website=NonStop Local Billings |language=en |date=2 March 2026 |quote=Congressman Ryan Zinke will not seek reelection for a fourth term in Montana's first Congressional District. The Republican who has had a storied career will make it official Monday as the filing deadline looms.}}</ref>
