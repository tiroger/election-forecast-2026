{{Elections in North Dakota sidebar}}
The '''2026 United States House of Representatives election in North Dakota''' is scheduled to be held on November 3, 2026, to elect a member of the [[United States House of Representatives]] to represent the [[U.S. state|state]] of [[North Dakota]] from its {{ushr|ND|AL|D}}. The election will coincide with [[2026 North Dakota elections|other elections]] held on the same date. The primary elections were held on June 9, 2026.<ref>{{Cite web |title=North Dakota 2026 General Election |url=https://www.thegreenpapers.com/G26/ND |access-date=2025-06-10 |website=thegreenpapers.com}}</ref>

[[Republican Party (United States)|Republican]] [[Julie Fedorchak]] was elected with 69.24% of the vote in 2024 against [[North Dakota Democratic–Nonpartisan League Party|Democratic–NPL]] challenger Trygve Hammer. It will be a rematch of the 2024 House of Representatives election.
