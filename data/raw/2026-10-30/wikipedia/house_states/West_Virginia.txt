{{Elections in West Virginia}}
The '''2026 United States House of Representatives elections in West Virginia''' will be held on November 3, 2026, to elect the 2 [[U.S. representative]]s from the [[U.S. state|State]] of [[West Virginia]], one from each of the state's [[West Virginia's congressional districts|congressional districts]]. The elections will coincide with [[2026 United States House of Representatives elections|other elections]] to the House of Representatives, [[2026 United States Senate election in West Virginia|elections]] to the [[United States Senate]], and various [[2026 United States elections|state and local elections]]. The primary elections took place on May 12, 2026.<ref>{{Cite web|date=May 9, 2025 |title=2026 State Primary Election Dates |url=https://www.ncsl.org/elections-and-campaigns/2026-state-primary-election-dates|website= NCSL |access-date=5 August 2025}}</ref>

{{see also|West Virginia's 1st congressional district}}
The 1st congressional district of West Virginia encompasses the southern portion of the state, including [[Charleston, West Virginia|Charleston]] and [[Huntington, West Virginia|Huntington]], as well as the smaller cities of [[Beckley, West Virginia|Beckley]], [[Bluefield, West Virginia|Bluefield]], [[Lewisburg, West Virginia|Lewisburg]], [[Princeton, West Virginia|Princeton]], and [[White Sulphur Springs, West Virginia|White Sulphur Springs]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.wvlegislature.gov/Bill_Status/Bills_history.cfm?input=3033&year=2021&sessiontype=3X&btype=bill |title = Bill Status - Complete Bill History}}</ref> The incumbent is Republican [[Carol Miller (politician)|Carol Miller]], who was re-elected with 66.4% of the vote in 2024.<ref name="HPVT">{{cite web|url=https://www.cookpolitical.com/vote-tracker/2024/house|title=2024 House Vote Tracker|access-date=February 25, 2025|website=[[The Cook Political Report with Amy Walter|Cook Political Report]]}}</ref>
