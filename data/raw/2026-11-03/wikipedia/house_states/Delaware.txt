The '''2026 United States House of Representatives election in Delaware''' will be held on November 3, 2026, to elect a member of the [[United States House of Representatives]] to represent the [[U.S. state|state]] of [[Delaware]] from its {{ushr|DE|AL|D}}. The election coincides with [[2026 United States House of Representatives elections|other elections]] to the U.S. House, [[2026 United States Senate elections|elections]] to the [[United States Senate]], and various other [[2026 United States elections|state and local elections]]. Primary elections took place on September 15, 2026.<ref>{{Cite web|date=May 9, 2025 |title=2026 State Primary Election Dates |url=https://www.ncsl.org/elections-and-campaigns/2026-state-primary-election-dates|website= NCSL |access-date=5 August 2025}}</ref>

Incumbent [[Democratic Party (United States)|Democratic]] representative [[Sarah McBride]] was elected in [[2024 United States House of Representatives election in Delaware|2024]] with 57.9% of the vote.<ref name="HPVT">{{cite web|url=https://www.cookpolitical.com/vote-tracker/2024/house|title=2024 House Vote Tracker|access-date=December 2, 2024 |website=[[The Cook Political Report with Amy Walter|Cook Political Report]]}}</ref>
