{{ElectionsMI}}
The '''2026 United States House of Representatives elections in Michigan''' will be held on November 3, 2026, to elect the 13 [[U.S. representative]]s from the state of [[Michigan]], one from all the state's [[Michigan's congressional districts|congressional districts]]. The elections will coincide with [[2026 United States House of Representatives elections|other elections]] to the House of Representatives, [[2026 United States Senate elections|elections]] to the [[United States Senate]], and various [[2026 United States elections|state and local elections]]. The primary elections took place on August 4, 2026. The filing deadline to run for the August 4 primary was April 21.<ref>https://mi-boe.entellitrak.com/etk-mi-boe-prod/page.request.do?page=page.miboePublicReport&electionType=PRI&electionYear=2026</ref>

The 1st district covers the [[Upper Peninsula of Michigan|Upper Peninsula]] and the northern part of the [[Lower Peninsula of Michigan|Lower Peninsula]], including [[Alpena, Michigan|Alpena]] and [[Traverse City, Michigan|Traverse City]]. The incumbent is Republican [[Jack Bergman]], who was re-elected with 59.1% of the vote in 2024.<ref name="HPVT">{{cite web|url=https://www.cookpolitical.com/vote-tracker/2024/house|title=2024 House Vote Tracker|access-date=December 2, 2024 |website=[[The Cook Political Report with Amy Walter|Cook Political Report]]}}</ref>

The 8th district centers around the [[Saginaw Bay]] and includes the cities of [[Flint, Michigan|Flint]], [[Saginaw, Michigan|Saginaw]], [[Bay City, Michigan|Bay City]], and [[Midland, Michigan|Midland]]. The incumbent is Democrat [[Kristen McDonald Rivet]], who was elected with 51.3% of the vote in 2024.<ref name="HPVT"/>

The 11th district is based solely in [[Oakland County, Michigan|Oakland County]] and includes the cities of [[Royal Oak, Michigan|Royal Oak]] and [[Pontiac, Michigan|Pontiac]]. The incumbent is Democrat [[Haley Stevens]], who was re-elected with 58.2% of the vote in 2024.<ref name="HPVT"/> Stevens did not seek re-election, instead choosing to [[2026 United States Senate election in Michigan|run for U.S. Senate]], where she would ultimately lose in the primary to [[Abdul El-Sayed]] by less than one percentage point.<ref name="Stevens"/>
