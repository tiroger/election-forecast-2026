{{ElectionsNJ}}
The '''2026 United States House of Representatives elections in New Jersey''' will be held on November 3, 2026, to elect the 12 [[U.S. representative]]s from the [[U.S. state|state]] of [[New Jersey]], one from all 12 of the state's [[New Jersey's congressional districts|congressional districts]]. The elections will coincide with [[2026 United States House of Representatives elections|other elections]] to the House of Representatives, [[2026 United States Senate elections|elections]] to the [[United States Senate]], and various [[2026 United States elections|state and local elections]]. The primary elections took place on June 2, 2026.<ref>{{Cite web|date=May 9, 2025 |title=2026 State Primary Election Dates |url=https://www.ncsl.org/elections-and-campaigns/2026-state-primary-election-dates|website=NCSL |access-date=August 5, 2025}}</ref>

{{see also|New Jersey's 1st congressional district}}
The 1st district consists of the [[South Jersey]] suburbs of [[Philadelphia]], including the municipalities of [[Camden, New Jersey|Camden]] and [[Cherry Hill, New Jersey|Cherry Hill]]. The incumbent is Democrat [[Donald Norcross]], who was re-elected with 57.8% of the vote in 2024.<ref name="HPVT" />

{{see also|New Jersey's 2nd congressional district}}
The 2nd district covers the majority of South Jersey, spanning from the [[Philadelphia metropolitan area]] to the upper [[New Jersey Pine Barrens|Pine Barrens]], taking in [[Atlantic City, New Jersey|Atlantic City]] and [[Vineland, New Jersey|Vineland]]. The incumbent is Republican [[Jeff Van Drew]], who was re-elected with 58.1% of the vote in 2024.<ref name="HPVT" />

The incumbent was Democrat [[Mikie Sherrill]], who was re-elected with 56.5% of the vote in 2024.<ref name="HPVT" /> The seat became vacant on November 20, 2025, after Sherrill won the [[2025 New Jersey gubernatorial election|2025 election]] for governor of New Jersey.<ref>{{cite news |last=Friedman |first=Matt |date=November 18, 2024 |title=New Jersey Rep. [[Analilia Mejia]] won the special election to fill the remainder of Sherrill's term, and has declared intention to seek a full term this November.<ref>{{cite news |last=Fox |first=Joey |date=November 21, 2025 |title=Murphy sets February 5 primary, April 16 general for NJ-11 special election |url=https://newjerseyglobe.com/congress/murphy-sets-february-5-primary-april-16-general-for-nj-11-special-election/ |access-date=November 21, 2025 |website=New Jersey Globe |language=en-US}}</ref>

The 12th district is composed of much of [[Central Jersey]], taking in the state capital [[Trenton, New Jersey|Trenton]] and neighboring [[Princeton University]], along with [[Plainfield, New Jersey|Plainfield]] to the north. The incumbent is Democrat [[Bonnie Watson Coleman]], who was re-elected with 61.2% of the vote in 2024.<ref name="HPVT" />
