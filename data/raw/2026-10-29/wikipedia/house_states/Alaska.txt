The '''2026 United States House of Representatives election in Alaska''' will be held on November 3, 2026, to elect a member of the [[United States House of Representatives]] to represent the [[U.S. state|state]] of [[Alaska]] and its [[Alaska's at-large congressional district|at-large district]]. The election will coincide with [[2026 United States House of Representatives elections|other elections]] to the U.S. House, [[2026 United States Senate elections|elections]] to the [[United States Senate]], and various other [[2026 United States elections|state and local elections]]. The primary election occurred on August 18, 2026.<ref>{{cite web|title = Primary Election Information|url = https://www.elections.alaska.gov/Core/primaryelectioninformation.php|website = Alaska Division of Elections|access-date = 2024-12-15|quote = Primary elections are held on the third Tuesday in August of even numbered years.|archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20220504030110/https://www.elections.alaska.gov/Core/primaryelectioninformation.php|archive-date = May 4, 2022|url-status = dead}}</ref>

Incumbent [[Nick Begich III]] was elected with 51.22% of the vote in [[2024 United States House of Representatives election in Alaska|2024]], narrowly defeating then-incumbent [[Mary Peltola]], the first Democrat to represent Alaska in the House since [[1972 United States House of Representatives elections|1972]].<ref>{{cite web|last = Miller|first = Maya|title = Begich Defeats Peltola in Alaska, Flipping House Seat for Republicans|url = https://www.nytimes.com/2024/11/20/us/elections/alaska-house-begich-peltola.html|website = The New York Times|date = 2024-11-20|access-date = 2024-12-15}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|access-date=2024-12-15 |date=2024-11-21 |first=Becky |last=Bohrer |title=Trump-backed Republican Nick Begich beats Democratic Rep. Mary Peltola for Alaska's only House seat |url=https://apnews.com/article/peltola-begich-alaska-house-e57bf9cf11a2668ffe67e40d840979f2 |website=AP News}}</ref><ref name=":0">{{cite web|last = Stone|first = Eric|title = Begich maintains lead and repeal of ranked choice still passing in updated election results|url = https://alaskapublic.org/2024/11/12/begich-maintains-lead-and-repeal-of-ranked-choice-still-passing-in-updated-election-results/|website = Alaska Public Media|date = 2024-11-12|access-date = 2024-12-15}}</ref>

The race will be conducted using [[instant-runoff voting|ranked-choice voting]]. The four candidates to advance from the August 18 [[nonpartisan primary]] and appear on the ballot for the November 3 general election were Begich, independent former [[Bristol Bay Borough, Alaska|Bristol Bay Borough]] School District superintendent Bill Hill, Democratic [[perennial candidate]] and federal prisoner [[Eric Hafner]], and Libertarian business professor Jim McDermott.

==General election==
===Campaign===
After the primary, Democrat Matt Schultz—who had announced the suspension of his campaign weeks earlier—formally withdrew, as did Democrat John Williams, Republican Clay Strickland, and Democrat Yaquelin Reynoso. Schultz, Williams, Strickland, and Reynoso had placed third, fifth, sixth, and seventh in the primary, respectively. This left Libertarian candidate Jim McDermott as the fourth candidate on the general election ballot despite him having placed eighth in the primary. Williams confirmed that his decision to withdraw had come after conversations with Hill and [[Alaska Democratic Party]] representatives.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Brooks |first1=James |title=In Alaska’s U.S. House race, a Libertarian and a federal inmate advance after late withdrawals |url=https://alaskabeacon.com/2026/08/31/in-alaskas-u-s-house-race-two-of-the-final-candidates-dont-live-in-alaska-and-one-is-in-prison/ |access-date=September 1, 2026 |work=[[Alaska Beacon]] |date=August 31, 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260901223330/https://alaskabeacon.com/2026/08/31/in-alaskas-u-s-house-race-two-of-the-final-candidates-dont-live-in-alaska-and-one-is-in-prison/ |archive-date=September 1, 2026 |url-status=live}}</ref>
