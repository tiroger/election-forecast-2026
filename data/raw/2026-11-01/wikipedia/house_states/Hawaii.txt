The '''2026 United States House of Representatives elections in Hawaii''' will be held on November 3, 2026, to elect the two [[U.S. representative]]s from the [[U.S. state|State]] of [[Hawaii]], one from both of the state's [[Hawaii's congressional districts|congressional districts]]. The elections will coincide with the [[2026 United States House of Representatives elections|other elections]] to the House of Representatives, [[2026 United States Senate elections|elections]] to the [[United States Senate]], and various [[2026 United States elections|state and local elections]]. The primary elections took place on August 8, 2026.<ref>{{Cite web|date=May 9, 2025 |title=2026 State Primary Election Dates |url=https://www.ncsl.org/elections-and-campaigns/2026-state-primary-election-dates|website= NCSL |access-date=5 August 2025}}</ref>

{{see also|Hawaii's 1st congressional district}}
The 1st district is located entirely on the island of [[Oahu]], centering on [[Honolulu]] and the towns of [[Aiea, Hawaii|Aiea]], [[Mililani, Hawaii|Mililani]], [[Pearl City, Hawaii|Pearl City]], [[Waipahu, Hawaii|Waipahu]], and [[Waimalu, Hawaii|Waimalu]]. The incumbent is Democrat [[Ed Case]], who was re-elected with 71.8% of the vote in 2024.<ref name="HPVT">{{cite web|url=https://www.cookpolitical.com/vote-tracker/2024/house|title=2024 House Vote Tracker|access-date=February 26, 2025|website=[[The Cook Political Report with Amy Walter|Cook Political Report]]}}</ref>

The 2nd district takes in rural and suburban [[Oahu]], including [[Waimanalo Beach, Hawaii|Waimanalo Beach]], [[Kailua, Hawaii|Kailua]], [[Kaneohe, Hawaii|Kaneohe]], [[Kahuku, Hawaii|Kahuku]], [[Makaha, Hawaii|Makaha]], [[Nanakuli, Hawaii|Nanakuli]], as well as encompassing all the other islands of Hawaii; taking in the counties of [[Hawaii County, Hawaii|Hawaii]], [[Kalawao County, Hawaii|Kalawao]], [[Kauaʻi County, Hawaii|Kauaʻi]], and [[Maui County, Hawaii|Maui]]. The incumbent is Democrat [[Jill Tokuda]], who was re-elected with 66.5% of the vote in 2024.<ref name="HPVT" />
