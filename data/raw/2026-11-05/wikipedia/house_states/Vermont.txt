The '''2026 United States House of Representatives election in Vermont''' will be held on November 3, 2026, to elect a member of the [[United States House of Representatives]] to represent [[Vermont]]'s [[Vermont's at-large congressional district|at-large district]]. [[Democratic Party (United States)|Democratic]] incumbent [[Becca Balint]] is seeking a third term. She is being challenged by [[Republican Party (United States)|Republican]] former military officer Gerald Malloy.

[[Primary elections]] took place on August 11, 2026.<ref>{{Cite web|title=2026 State Primary Election Dates|url=https://www.ncsl.org/elections-and-campaigns/2026-state-primary-election-dates|website=[[National Conference of State Legislatures]]|date=May 9, 2025|access-date=August 5, 2025}}</ref> In the Democratic primary, Balint secured 98.4% of the vote against [[write-in]] opposition. Malloy defeated [[perennial candidate]] Mark Coester in the Republican primary with 76.3% of the vote.
