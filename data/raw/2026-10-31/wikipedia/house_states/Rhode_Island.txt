[[Elections]] will be held on November 3, 2026, to elect the two [[U.S. representative]]s from the state of [[Rhode Island]], one from both of the state's [[Rhode Island's congressional districts|congressional districts]]. [[Primary elections]] took place on September 9, 2026.<ref>{{Cite web|title=2026 State Primary Election Dates|url=https://www.ncsl.org/elections-and-campaigns/2026-state-primary-election-dates|date=May 9, 2025|publisher=[[National Conference of State Legislatures]]|access-date=August 5, 2025}}</ref>

{{see also|Rhode Island's 1st congressional district}}
The 1st district encompasses the eastern portion of the state, including [[Pawtucket, Rhode Island|Pawtucket]], [[Woonsocket, Rhode Island|Woonsocket]], [[Newport, Rhode Island|Newport]], and much of [[Providence, Rhode Island|Providence]]. The incumbent is Democrat [[Gabe Amo]], who was re-elected with 63.0% of the vote in 2024.<ref name="HPVT">{{cite web|url=https://www.cookpolitical.com/vote-tracker/2024/house|title=2024 House Vote Tracker|access-date=February 26, 2025|website=[[The Cook Political Report]]}}</ref>
