{{Elections in Iowa}}
The '''2026 United States House of Representatives elections in Iowa''' will be held on November 3, 2026, to elect the four [[U.S. representative]]s from the state of [[Iowa]], one from each of the state's [[Iowa's congressional districts|congressional districts]]. The elections will coincide with [[2026 United States House of Representatives elections|other elections]] to the House of Representatives, [[2026 United States Senate elections|elections]] to the [[United States Senate]], and various [[2026 United States elections|state and local elections]]. The primary elections took place on June 2, 2026.<ref>{{Cite web|date=May 9, 2025 |title=2026 State Primary Election Dates |url=https://www.ncsl.org/elections-and-campaigns/2026-state-primary-election-dates|publisher=NCSL |access-date=5 August 2025}}</ref>

This district is based in the southeastern part of the state and includes the cities of [[Davenport, Iowa|Davenport]] and [[Iowa City, Iowa|Iowa City]]. The incumbent is Republican [[Mariannette Miller-Meeks]], who was re-elected with 50.0% of the vote in 2024.<ref name="HPVT">{{cite web|url=https://www.cookpolitical.com/vote-tracker/2024/house|title=2024 House Vote Tracker|access-date=December 2, 2024 |website=[[The Cook Political Report with Amy Walter|Cook Political Report]]}}</ref> This win was the narrowest of any incumbent House Republican in 2024.

The election was one of the most expensive congressional races in the 2026 elections, with over $51.5 million in spending.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Schwartz |first=Berl |date=2026-10-08 |title=$51.5 million later, Michigan’s 7th is the nation’s second-most expensive House race |url=https://www.wkar.org/election-2026/2026-10-08/51-5-million-later-michigans-7th-is-the-nations-second-most-expensive-house-race |access-date=2026-10-15 |website=WKAR Public Media |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Mutnick |first=Ally |date=October 6, 2026 |title=The blockbuster House races |url=https://punchbowl.news/article/campaigns/blockbuster-house-races/ |access-date=2026-10-15 |website=Punchbowl News |language=en-US}}</ref>
