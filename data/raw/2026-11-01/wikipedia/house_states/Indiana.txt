{{Elections in Indiana}}
The '''2026 United States House of Representatives elections in Indiana''' will be held on November 3, 2026, to elect the nine [[U.S. representative]]s from the [[U.S. state|state]] of [[Indiana]], one from each of the state's [[Indiana's congressional districts|congressional districts]]. The elections will coincide with [[2026 United States House of Representatives elections|other elections]] to the House of Representatives, [[2026 United States Senate elections|elections]] to the [[United States Senate]], and various [[2026 United States elections|state and local elections]]. The primary elections took place on May 5, 2026.<ref>{{Cite web|date=May 9, 2025 |title=2026 State Primary Election Dates |url=https://www.ncsl.org/elections-and-campaigns/2026-state-primary-election-dates|website= NCSL |access-date=5 August 2025}}</ref>

== Failed redistricting attempt ==
In late 2025, as part of the broader [[2025–2026 United States redistricting|2025 U.S. redistricting cycle]], [[President of the United States|President]] [[Donald Trump]] urged Indiana to redraw its congressional map to unseat Democratic Representatives [[Frank J. Mrvan|Frank Mrvan]] (1st district) and [[André Carson]] (7th district). The proposal faced unexpected resistance in the Republican-[[supermajority]] [[Indiana Senate|Senate]], with more than a dozen Republicans opposing it. Trump sought to pressure holdouts by threatening to back primary challengers against those who voted no.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Pellish |first=Aaron |date=2025-12-10 |title=Trump threatens to primary Indiana Republicans ahead of key redistricting vote |url=https://www.politico.com/news/2025/12/10/trump-indiana-redistricting-republicans-rodric-bray-00686257 |access-date=2025-12-11 |website=POLITICO |language=en}}</ref> Separately, several senators received threats, including [[bomb threat]]s and [[Swatting|swatting calls]], ahead of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web |last1=Uribe |first1=Raquel Coronell |last2=Lebowitz |first2=Megan |date=2025-12-01 |title=At least 11 Indiana Republicans were targeted with threats or swatting attacks amid redistricting pressure from Trump |url=https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-election/indiana-republicans-swatting-attacks-redistricting-rcna246689 |access-date=2025-12-11 |website=NBC News |language=en}}</ref> The measure passed the [[Indiana House of Representatives|House]] 57–41 on December 5, with twelve Republicans joining all Democrats in opposition,<ref>{{Cite web |last1=Muñiz |first1=Leslie Bonilla |last2=Davies |first2=Tom |date=2025-12-05 |title=Indiana House approves redistricting bill, sending issue to state Senate • Indiana Capital Chronicle |url=https://indianacapitalchronicle.com/2025/12/05/indiana-house-approves-redistricting-bill-sending-issue-to-state-senate/ |access-date=2025-12-11 |website=Indiana Capital Chronicle |language=en-US}}</ref> but failed in the Senate on December 11 by a 31–19 vote, as 21 Republicans joined all 10 Democrats to defeat it.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Vakil |first=Caroline |date=December 11, 2025 |title=Indiana Senate rejects new House map, defying Trump |url=https://thehill.com/homenews/campaign/5644276-indiana-senate-rejects-gop-map/ |access-date=December 11, 2025 |website=[[The Hill (newspaper)|The Hill]]}}</ref>

The 1st district encompasses [[Northwest Indiana]], taking in the eastern [[Chicago metropolitan area]], including [[Hammond, Indiana|Hammond]] and [[Gary, Indiana|Gary]], as well as [[Lake County, Indiana|Lake County]], [[Porter County, Indiana|Porter County]] and northwest [[LaPorte County, Indiana|LaPorte County]]. The incumbent is Democrat [[Frank J. Mrvan|Frank Mrvan]], who was re-elected with 53.4% of the vote in 2024.<ref name="HPVT">{{cite web|url=https://www.cookpolitical.com/vote-tracker/2024/house|title=2024 House Vote Tracker|access-date=December 2, 2024 |website=[[The Cook Political Report with Amy Walter|Cook Political Report]]}}</ref>
