The '''2026 United States House of Representatives elections in New Hampshire''' will be held on November 3, 2026, to elect the two [[U.S. representative]]s from the [[U.S. state|state]] of [[New Hampshire]], one from both of the state's [[New Hampshire's congressional districts|congressional districts]]. The elections will coincide with the [[2026 United States House of Representatives elections|other elections]] to the House of Representatives, [[2026 United States Senate elections|elections]] to the [[United States Senate]], and various [[2026 United States elections|state and local elections]]. The primary election took place on September 8, 2026.<ref>{{Cite web|date=May 9, 2025 |title=2026 State Primary Election Dates |url=https://www.ncsl.org/elections-and-campaigns/2026-state-primary-election-dates|website= NCSL |access-date=5 August 2025}}</ref>

{{see also|New Hampshire's 1st congressional district}}
The 1st district is based in southeastern New Hampshire, and includes Greater [[Manchester, New Hampshire|Manchester]], the [[Seacoast Region (New Hampshire)|Seacoast]] and the [[Lakes Region (New Hampshire)|Lakes Region]]. The incumbent is Democrat [[Chris Pappas (American politician)|Chris Pappas]], who was re-elected with 54.0% of the vote in 2024.<ref name="HPVT">{{cite web|url=https://www.cookpolitical.com/vote-tracker/2024/house|title=2024 House Vote Tracker|access-date=February 25, 2025|website=[[The Cook Political Report with Amy Walter|Cook Political Report]]}}</ref> Pappas is retiring to [[2026 United States Senate election in New Hampshire|run for Senate in 2026]].<ref name=pappas/>
