The '''2026 United States House of Representatives elections in Minnesota''' will be held on November 3, 2026, to elect the 8 [[United States House of Representatives|U.S. representatives]] from the [[U.S. state|state]] of [[Minnesota]], one from each of the state's [[Minnesota's congressional districts|congressional districts]]. The elections will coincide with [[2026 United States House of Representatives elections|other elections]] to the House of Representatives, [[2026 United States Senate elections|elections]] to the [[United States Senate]], and various [[2026 United States elections|state and local elections]]. Primary elections were held August 11, 2026.<ref name="calendar">{{cite web|title=Elections Calendar|url=https://www.sos.state.mn.us/election-administration-campaigns/elections-calendar/|website=Minnesota Secretary of State|access-date=February 14, 2025}}</ref>

{{see also|Minnesota's 1st congressional district}}
The 1st district stretches across southern Minnesota from its borders with [[South Dakota]] to [[Wisconsin]], and includes the cities of [[Rochester, Minnesota|Rochester]], [[Mankato, Minnesota|Mankato]], [[Winona, Minnesota|Winona]], [[Austin, Minnesota|Austin]], [[Owatonna, Minnesota|Owatonna]], [[Albert Lea, Minnesota|Albert Lea]], [[New Ulm, Minnesota|New Ulm]], and [[Worthington, Minnesota|Worthington]]. The incumbent is Republican [[Brad Finstad]], who was re-elected with 58.5% of the vote in 2024. Democrats have not won this seat in an election since 2016.

{{see also|Minnesota's 2nd congressional district}}
The 2nd district is based in the southern [[Twin Cities]] suburbs, including [[Burnsville, Minnesota|Burnsville]], [[Eagan, Minnesota|Eagan]], and [[Lakeville, Minnesota|Lakeville]]. The incumbent is Democrat [[Angie Craig]], who was re-elected with 55.5% of the vote in 2024. Craig retired to [[2026 United States Senate election in Minnesota|run for U.S. Senate in 2026]], losing to Lieutenant Governor Peggy Flanagan in the August 11, 2026, DFL primary election.<ref name="Craig"/>

{{see also|Minnesota's 3rd congressional district}}
The 3rd district encompasses the western suburbs of the Twin Cities, including [[Brooklyn Park, Minnesota|Brooklyn Park]], [[Coon Rapids, Minnesota|Coon Rapids]] to the northeast, [[Bloomington, Minnesota|Bloomington]] to the south, and [[Eden Prairie, Minnesota|Eden Prairie]], [[Edina, Minnesota|Edina]], [[Maple Grove, Minnesota|Maple Grove]], [[Plymouth, Minnesota|Plymouth]], [[Minnetonka, Minnesota|Minnetonka]], and [[Wayzata, Minnesota|Wayzata]] to the west. The incumbent is Democrat [[Kelly Morrison]], who was elected with 58.4% of the vote in 2024. Republicans have not won an election for this seat since 2016.

{{see also|Minnesota's 5th congressional district}}
The 5th district encompasses eastern [[Hennepin County, Minnesota|Hennepin County]], including all of [[Minneapolis, Minnesota|Minneapolis]] and the cities of [[St. Louis Park, Minnesota|St. Louis Park]], [[Richfield, Minnesota|Richfield]], [[Crystal, Minnesota|Crystal]], [[Robbinsdale, Minnesota|Robbinsdale]], [[Golden Valley, Minnesota|Golden Valley]], [[New Hope, Minnesota|New Hope]], and [[Fridley, Minnesota|Fridley]]. The incumbent is Democrat [[Ilhan Omar]], who was re-elected with 74.3% of the vote in 2024. Omar had expressed interest in [[2026 United States Senate election in Minnesota|running for U.S. Senate in 2026]], but ultimately announced in April 2025 that she would run for re-election.<ref name=axiosapr10/>

{{see also|Minnesota's 6th congressional district}}
The 6th district encompasses the northern suburbs and exurbs of [[Minneapolis]], including all of [[Benton County, Minnesota|Benton]], [[Sherburne County, Minnesota|Sherburne]], and [[Wright County, Minnesota|Wright]] counties and parts of [[Anoka County, Minnesota|Anoka]], [[Carver County, Minnesota|Carver]], [[Stearns County, Minnesota|Stearns]], and [[Washington County, Minnesota|Washington]] counties. The incumbent is Republican [[Tom Emmer]], who was re-elected with 62.0% of the vote in 2024. No Democrat has won an election for this seat since 1998.

The 7th district covers all but the southern end of rural western Minnesota, and includes the cities of [[Moorhead, Minnesota|Moorhead]], [[Willmar, Minnesota|Willmar]], [[Alexandria, Minnesota|Alexandria]], and [[Fergus Falls, Minnesota|Fergus Falls]]. The incumbent is Republican [[Michelle Fischbach]], who was re-elected with 70.5% of the vote in 2024.
