{{Elections in New Mexico}}
The '''2026 United States House of Representatives elections in New Mexico ''' will be held on November 3, 2026, to elect the three [[U.S. representative]]s from the [[U.S. state|state]] of [[New Mexico]], one from each of the state's [[New Mexico's congressional districts|congressional districts]]. The elections will coincide with [[2026 United States House of Representatives elections|other elections]] to the House of Representatives, [[2026 United States Senate elections|elections]] to the [[United States Senate]], and various [[2026 United States elections|state and local elections]]. The primary elections took place on June 2, 2026.<ref>{{Cite web|date=May 9, 2025 |title=2026 State Primary Election Dates |url=https://www.ncsl.org/elections-and-campaigns/2026-state-primary-election-dates|website= NCSL |access-date=5 August 2025}}</ref>

{{see also|New Mexico's 1st congressional district}}
The 1st district covers the center of the state, taking in the counties of [[Torrance County, New Mexico|Torrance]], [[Guadalupe County, New Mexico|Guadalupe]], [[De Baca County, New Mexico|De Baca]], and [[Lincoln County, New Mexico|Lincoln]], as well as eastern [[Bernalillo County, New Mexico|Bernalillo County]] and most of [[Albuquerque, New Mexico|Albuquerque]]. The incumbent is Democrat [[Melanie Stansbury]], who was re-elected with 56.4% of the vote in 2024.<ref name="HV"/>

{{see also|New Mexico's 2nd congressional district}}
The 2nd district encapsulates southern and western New Mexico, including the cities of [[Las Cruces, New Mexico|Las Cruces]], [[Carlsbad, New Mexico|Carlsbad]], and [[Alamogordo, New Mexico|Alamogordo]], as well as the southwestern suburbs of [[Albuquerque, New Mexico|Albuquerque]]. The incumbent is Democrat [[Gabe Vasquez]], who was re-elected with 52.1% of the vote in 2024.<ref name="HV"/>

{{see also|New Mexico's 3rd congressional district}}
The 3rd district covers the northern and eastern parts of the state, taking in the cities of [[Santa Fe, New Mexico|Santa Fe]], [[Roswell, New Mexico|Roswell]], [[Farmington, New Mexico|Farmington]], and [[Clovis, New Mexico|Clovis]], as well as parts of the [[Navajo Nation]]. The incumbent is Democrat [[Teresa Leger Fernández]], who was re-elected with 56.3% of the vote in 2024.<ref name="HV">{{Cite web |title=2024 House Vote Tracker |url=https://www.cookpolitical.com/vote-tracker/2024/house |access-date=2025-06-19 |website=Cook Political Report |language=en}}</ref>
